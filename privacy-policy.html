Privacy Policy

RanDOM WisDOM (Android) · Last updated 8 September 2026

  • RanDOM WisDOM is free, has no accounts and no sign-up. There are no adverts, no analytics and we never sell your data.
  • We never collect advertising IDs, GPS location, contacts, photos or files. The app has no microphone access.
  • Your meditation history stays on your phone.
  • We only receive anything if you turn on notifications or write to us.
  • Please note that the Discuss feature is provided by a third party and is subject to their privacy policy.

The app is provided by followCrom, United Kingdom, who is the data controller. Contact us via the Contact screen in the app or at followcrom.com/contact.

What we handle

Feature Data Stored
Meditation history meditation name, practice date, length Stored on your phone until you clear it in Settings or uninstall
Notifications push token, app version, your phone's language region Stored until you switch notifications off or uninstall
Discuss messages sent to DeepSeek to generate replies Not stored by us or on your phone. See the warning below
Contact form name, email, subject, message Stored until your enquiry is dealt with

The app runs on Amazon Web Services in London; Google and Expo deliver the notifications.

The Discuss feature

Don't type anything sensitive into Discuss. Your messages go to DeepSeek's servers in China; a country without a UK adequacy decision. DeepSeek may retain your messages and use them to train its models. You can review DeepSeek's privacy policy. Every other part of the app works without the Discuss feature.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for access to your data, or its correction or deletion, and can withdraw consent at any time. Since there are no accounts, we may not be able to link data to you from your name alone.

You can perform the following actions yourself:

  • Clear your history in Settings.
  • Switch notifications off to delete your token.

For anything else, contact us and we'll reply within a month.

You can also complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.

Children

The app isn't aimed at under 13s and we don't collect their data.

Changes

Updates appear on this page with a new date above. Anything that materially affects your data will be flagged in the app first.
