## Discover the power of perspective with **RanDOM WisDOM**.

<div style="font-size:18px;">
In today’s fast-paced world, it's all too easy to become overwhelmed by daily anxieties. Our app serves as a gentle reminder: things often aren't as bad as they seem. Whether you prefer soothing text or calming audio, we offer you short extracts of curated wisdom to uplift your spirits and declutter your mind. 🧘🏼‍♀️

<br>

Dip into brief, impactful quotes from celebrated philosophers and mindful minds. If you need a little more time to recentre, immerse yourself in our unique 10-minute meditations. Unlike traditional guided meditations, our sessions are filled with practical advice, offering actionable wisdom that stands the test of time. 🪷

<br>

✨ Key Features:

  <ul>
    <li>Uplifting quotes in text or audio. 🤍</li>
    <li>Specialized 10-minute meditations filled with philosophical guidance. 🍃</li>
    <li>An intuitive, user-friendly interface. 🌿</li>
    <li>Mindful notifications that bring moments of calm when you need them most. ☁️</li>
    <li>Interact with followCrom the Wise, our friendly chatbot, for a personalized experience. 🧙</li>
  </ul>

For your peace of mind, RanDOM WisDOM is 100% free, requires no sign-ups, and guarantees zero data collection.
